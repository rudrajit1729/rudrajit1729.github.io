<div style="text-align: justify;">
  I’m Rudrajit (Rudy), a fifth-year Ph.D. candidate at <a href="https://engineering.oregonstate.edu/EECS">Oregon State University</a>, advised by <a href="https://web.engr.oregonstate.edu/~sarmaa/">Dr. Anita Sarma</a>. I am a mixed-methods researcher working at the intersections of Human-Centered AI (HCAI), Software Engineering (SE), Cognitive Science, UX Design, CS Education, and the Future of Work. I study (1) <strong><em>the cognitive and socio-technical factors that shape human–AI collaboration</em></strong>, and (2) <strong><em>how to design human-centered interfaces for AI tools that improve AI-native knowledge work.</em></strong>

  <br><br>
  Most recently, I spent two summers at Microsoft: with <a href="https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/group/saintes-group/">Microsoft Research (SAINTES)</a> in 2025, studying where and why developers want AI support and how to design it responsibly (<a href="https://aka.ms/AI-Where-It-Matters">AI Where It Matters</a>), and with Microsoft Tech Futures (X3) in 2026, investigating <a href="https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.13786">what makes a great co-worker in an AI-native workplace</a> and translating human-centered AI research into a practitioner-facing <a href="https://aka.ms/design-cookbook">design cookbook for UI/UX of AI</a>.

  <br><br>
  My broader goal is to empower developers and knowledge workers with trustworthy, responsible, and inclusive AI support, so they can work more effectively, keep oversight and control over AI, rely on it appropriately, and retain their agency and craft. My work has appeared at leading SE/HCI venues (ICSE, TOSEM, ICSME, ICER, EMSE, and ACMQ) and Machine Learning and Computer Vision venues (CVIP, ICVGIP, Applied Soft Computing, and Neural Computing and Applications), and has received four distinguished paper awards.

  <br><br>
  I am grateful to have been mentored by incredible researchers who have shaped my journey: <a href="https://web.engr.oregonstate.edu/~sarmaa/">Anita Sarma</a> and <a href="https://web.engr.oregonstate.edu/~burnett/">Margaret Burnett</a> for SE+HCAI, <a href="https://www.igor.pro.br/">Igor Steinmacher</a> and <a href="https://biancatrink.github.io/">Bianca Trinkenreich</a> for SE, <a href="https://sites.google.com/view/casanchezphd">Christopher Sanchez</a> for Cognitive Psychology, <a href="https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/people/cbird/">Christian Bird</a> and <a href="https://www.ime.usp.br/~gerosa/">Marco Gerosa</a> for SE+AI.
