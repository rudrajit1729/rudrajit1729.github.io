I study the cognitive and socio-technical factors that shape human–AI collaboration, and design human-centered interfaces for AI tools that improve AI-native knowledge work.

My goal is AI support that is trustworthy, responsible, and inclusive, so people can rely on it appropriately while keeping their agency and craft.
